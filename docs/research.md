# Research workflow

The research pipeline is intentionally offline and reproducible. It reads frozen historical inputs, produces derived artifacts under `quant/outputs/`, and records compact manifests and reports under `quant/reports/`.

Research results are descriptive. They do not establish future profitability, exact intent recovery, or permission to trade.

## Boundary

Raw account exports remain read-only. Public market data is joined only with previous-or-equal UTC observations. Large derived datasets are local artifacts and are not required to be committed.
